But you don’t need to turn into some hustle-culture lunatic to do it.

You want to make a lot of money.

But you’d rather tell somebody **“your roof is fine”** than sell them a roof they don’t need.

You want to get exceptionally good at something hard.

You take coaching without getting defensive.

You keep your word.

You remember names.

You follow up.

You care whether the job actually went well after the paperwork is signed.

And when you come back six months later, they open the door and say:

**“Hey Jamie\! How’ve you been?”**

This job is hard.

At first, everybody you meet is a stranger.

But for you, a stranger is just a friend you haven’t met yet.
