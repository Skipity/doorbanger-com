Who we are and what this covers

Refundamental, a Wyoming corporation doing business as DoorBangers (“DoorBangers,” “we,” “us”), operates doorbangers.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit this website or contact us about it, our company, or recruiting.

This policy covers the public website and those communications. Our descriptions of software and future services do not mean this website collects the data those tools would use. Separate applications, employment systems, and project services require notices describing their own data practices.

Information we receive

When you email, call, or write to us, we receive the contact details and information you choose to provide. This can include your name, email address, phone number, the contents of your message, and any attachments. If you inquire about working with us, it can also include your résumé and work history. Please avoid sending Social Security numbers, payment card details, or other sensitive information in an ordinary inquiry.

To deliver website pages, our hosting infrastructure receives technical information such as your IP address, requested page, and browser request headers. Hosting and security providers may retain access logs containing timestamps, device or browser information, and referring pages. We use technical information to operate the website, investigate errors, and protect it against misuse.

The website does not offer account registration, online application forms, payment processing, or file uploads. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or precise device location.

Cookies and tracking

The website itself does not set cookies or use browser storage, advertising pixels, or analytics scripts. Fonts and images are served with the site. We do not use information collected through this website or related inquiries to sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

The site does not track your activity across other companies’ websites. Its behavior does not change in response to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals because it does not perform that tracking or advertising sharing.

How we use information

We use information you send us to answer you, consider recruiting inquiries, follow up on your request, and maintain records of our communications. We also use information when needed to protect the website, address complaints, comply with legal obligations, or establish and defend legal claims.

Contacting us does not sign you up for an advertising mailing list or authorize automated marketing calls or texts.

When information is shared

Hosting, email, telephone, and other service providers process information as needed to deliver their services to us. People handling recruiting inquiries or your request may access the information relevant to their work. We require providers handling personal information on our behalf to protect it and use it only for the authorized services.

We may disclose information to professional advisers or authorities when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, investigate misuse, or protect people and legal rights. If the business is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, relevant records may be transferred under protections consistent with this policy. We may also share information at your direction.

Retention and security

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any related follow-up. Recruiting information is retained for the recruiting process and related recordkeeping obligations. Technical logs are kept as needed for website operations and security. Retention depends on the purpose of the record, whether an issue remains open, and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer needed for those purposes, we delete it or remove identifying details. Records needed for a legal obligation, an active dispute, or a security investigation may be retained until that need ends. Backup copies may remain until replaced through the provider’s backup cycle.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, including limiting access to people who need it for their work. No method of internet transmission or storage guarantees complete security.

Privacy and deletion requests

You can ask to access, correct, or delete personal information you have provided, or ask us to stop optional follow-up. Email privacy@doorbangers.com, call 234-228-6282, or write to the address below. For deletion, use the subject “DoorBangers deletion request” and tell us which correspondence or information your request concerns. Contact us from the email address you previously used when possible. Do not send passwords or government identification with your initial request.

We may ask for enough information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any information we must retain and why. The current website has no user accounts to delete.

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have additional rights to receive a copy of your information, use an authorized agent, or appeal a request decision. Send an appeal to the same privacy address with the subject “Privacy appeal.” We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Adults only

This website is intended for people who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We will post revisions on this page and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we handle personal information, we will provide additional notice and obtain consent when required by applicable law.
