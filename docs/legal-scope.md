## App-store follow-up depends on the app

The owner was asked whether these documents also cover an iOS/Android application. App data practices have not yet been supplied. Do not submit this website-only policy as if it covers an app with additional data collection.

Before an app submission, inventory account details, location (including background access), microphone recordings, photos, homeowner/job information, payments, identifiers, crash reports, analytics, SDKs, AI providers, recipients, purposes, retention, and deletion. Extend the policy to those verified practices. Include required prominent in-app disclosures and consent; a policy link alone does not supply them.

Apple requires an accessible policy in the app and App Store Connect, accurate privacy disclosures, and in-app initiation of account deletion where account creation is supported. An ordinary email-to-support link is not a substitute for that in-app flow. Disclosures must describe third-party protections, retention, and deletion. Review any third-party AI data sharing and required express permission.

Google requires a public HTML privacy policy linked in the app and Play Console, accurate Data safety declarations, and, for apps supporting account creation, an in-app deletion route plus a working external account-and-data deletion resource. Associated account data must be deleted, subject to disclosed legitimate retention exceptions.

App licensing, subscriptions, purchase terms, and any Apple custom EULA requirements need review against the actual app. Website Terms and Conditions do not by themselves supply an app license or store approval.
